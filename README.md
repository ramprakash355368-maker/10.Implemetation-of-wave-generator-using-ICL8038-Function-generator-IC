# 10.Implemetation-of-wave-generator-using-ICL8038-Function-generator-IC
**Aim:**
To Simulate Triangular-wave-generator using Op-amp

**SOFTWARE REQUIRED:**
 Proteus

**PROCEDURE:**
1.Open Proteus ISIS and create a new schematic project.

2.From Pick Devices, search and place ICL8038 (or the available equivalent function-generator IC in your Proteus library).

3.Place the required resistors, potentiometer, capacitor, DC power supply, and GND components according to the ICL8038 circuit diagram.

4.Connect the timing resistor and capacitor to the appropriate timing terminals of the ICL8038 to determine the oscillation frequency.

5.Connect the positive and negative power supplies as required by the selected ICL8038 configuration, and connect the circuit ground.

6.Connect Oscilloscope/CRO channels to the sine-wave, triangular-wave, and square-wave output pins.

7.Set the required component values and start the simulation by clicking the Play button.

8.Observe the sine, triangular, and square waveforms on the oscilloscope.

9.Vary the potentiometer/timing resistance and observe the corresponding change in output frequency.

10.Record the waveform shape, frequency, and amplitude from the virtual oscilloscope.
